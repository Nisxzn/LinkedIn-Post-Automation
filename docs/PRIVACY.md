# Comprehensive Privacy Policy

**Version 2.0 | Effective Date: May 6, 2026**

LinkedAI ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our LinkedIn Post Automation service (the "Service").

---

## 1. Information Collection and Tracking

### 1.1 Personal Data
While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"). This includes, but is not limited to:
- **Email Address**: Used for account identification, notifications, and security alerts.
- **Full Name**: Used to personalize your dashboard experience.
- **LinkedIn Profile Data**: Accessed via OAuth2, including your LinkedIn URN, profile picture URL, and name.

### 1.2 Usage and Technical Data
We collect information that your browser sends whenever you visit our Service:
- **IP Address**: Collected for security monitoring and regional analytics.
- **Browser Type and Version**: To ensure compatibility and optimize performance.
- **Pages Visited and Time Spent**: To understand user behavior and improve UI/UX.

### 1.3 Content Data
We store the content you generate and schedule through our Service, including:
- **Post Drafts**: Stored in our database until deleted by the user.
- **Scheduled Times**: To facilitate the automation process.
- **Analytics Snapshots**: Likes, comments, and impressions data fetched from LinkedIn to populate your dashboard.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes:
- **To provide and maintain the Service**: Ensuring your scheduled posts are delivered on time.
- **To notify you about changes**: Keeping you updated on new features or service interruptions.
- **To provide customer support**: Accessing logs to troubleshoot issues you report.
- **To gather analysis or valuable information**: So that we can improve the Service.
- **To monitor the usage of the Service**: Detecting, preventing, and addressing technical issues.
- **AI Optimization**: Using anonymized content patterns to improve the quality of AI-generated post structures (we do NOT sell your data to third-party AI trainers).

---

## 3. Data Storage and Transfer

### 3.1 Security of Data
The security of your data is important to us. We use **AES-256 encryption** for sensitive data at rest and **TLS 1.3** for all data in transit. 

### 3.2 Third-Party Service Providers
We employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Service:
- **OpenAI**: Content generation is processed via OpenAI's API. Please note that data sent to OpenAI is subject to their API privacy policies.
- **LinkedIn API**: All posting and analytics fetching is done through official LinkedIn developer endpoints.
- **Cloud Infrastructure**: Our servers are hosted on secure, industry-leading cloud providers with high physical and digital security standards.

---

## 4. Your Rights Under GDPR/CCPA

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:
- **The right to access**: Request copies of your personal data.
- **The right to rectification**: Request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
- **The right to erasure**: Request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
- **The right to data portability**: Request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization.
